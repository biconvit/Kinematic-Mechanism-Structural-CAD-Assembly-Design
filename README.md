# Kinematic-Mechanism-Structural-CAD-Assembly-Design
- Designed a dynamic counterweight pivoting mechanism in SolidWorks capable of launching payloads over 5 meters, performing rotational kinetic energy (0.5*I*ω²) and lever arm ratio optimization (3.67:1).
- Analyzed contact friction and force equilibrium on the release pin to mathematically derive the optimal release angle (51° bend for a 45° launch trajectory).
- Applied Design for Manufacturing (DFM) across hybrid fabrication processes: engineered 3D-printed structural base mounts with tight assembly slots and laser-cut acrylic A-frame uprights featuring truss-like cutouts to minimize weight and inertial overturning moments.
- Implemented precise machining and assembly tolerances (0.1 mm clearance fits) across fulcrum shafts, pivot bushings, and multi-part bolted joints to ensure smooth rotational freedom and structural rigidity.
- Produced a complete engineering drafting package adhering to technical standards, including Bill of Materials (BOM), exploded views, and detailed 2D fabrication drawings with orthographic projections, section cuts (Section A-A, B-B), and dimensional tolerancing.
